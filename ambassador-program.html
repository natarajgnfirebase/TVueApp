HPE Ambassador Program

Welcome to the HPE Ambassador Community for Partner Solution Architects! You have come highly recommended by your management in providing feedback through this community to HPE Business Units to help shape roadmap discussions on HPE products and solutions. As one of the top partners in the world, you are eligible to attend the annual Worldwide Ambassador Summit and we facilitate monthly calls offering access to restricted or deeper knowledge presented by global HPE executives and product managers.

For those new to the program, please review the Partner Ambassador Program 2020 Launch call

Expectations

  • Evangelize
  • HPE products and solutions-with customers and peers
  • Be the 'go-to' person within your organization to promote, share knowledge and advise on HPE products and solutions
  • Represent
  • Represent your company, business, country and share knowledge with peers within your company
  • Bring "street intelligence" into focus by voicing the feedback gained from your customers and personal experience
  • Engage
  • Attend and actively participate in all monthly ambassador calls - live or replay within 1 month
  • Engage in ongoing information exchange within Tech Pro Community's WW Partner Ambassadors group and share experiences with fellow Ambassadors
  • Contribute via ad-hoc surveys from BUs and program leads
  • Present constructive feedback as 'problem statement and a suggested solution'

Engagement

Ongoing engagement occurs in 3 pillars:

  • Partner Ambassador Private Group
  • Active continual participation, ask questions and assist fellow Partner Ambassadors
  • Monthly calls
  • Attend Monthly Calls (live or via on-demand replay within the quarter)
  • Ask questions, provide feedback to the presenters
  • At Worldwide Ambassador Summit (WWAS)
  • Prior to conference, consult your network on HPE products and strategy to obtain their feedback
  • While at the conference, be active in discussions
  • Provide feedback for every session via conference app
  • Grow your network, ask for special meetings
  • Bring home insights to share with co-workers and clients to further your HPE Sales

Your Partner Ambassador Geo Leads

Contact your local Partner Ambassador Geo Leads at any time. We are here to facilitate and drive your business.

Onboarding Checklist

Please complete the onboarding checklist:

  • Join Tech Pro Community
  • Join Tech Pro Forum
  • Join Ambassador Forum
    • Subscribe to Ambassador Forum updates via "Subscribe to this group by email" link on web page
  • Confirm Monthly Call Library access
  • Request confidential Pre-release NPI Content
  • Report back to your Geo Lead

Benefits
